Kya aapki bhi thand ke mausam mein subah uthte hi ya raat ko soote samay pair ki nas chadh jati hai? Is teez dard ki wajah se subah ka mood kharab ho jata hai. 1. Din bhar mein kam se kam 8-10 glass gunaguna paani zaroor piyein.
2. Raat ko soone se pehle 5 minute leg stretching karein aur saarso ke tel se halki malish karein.
3. Apni diet mein kela, til, aur hara saag shamil karein taaki shareer ko potassium aur magnesium mile.
